Nabil Karim Abdurrahman Bandung, ID · Class of 2026

Driven by a passion for creating useful, impactful solutions. A Physics Engineering undergrad at ITB exploring embedded control systems and mechatronics — systems built from the circuit level up.

Long-term: deeptech research, precision systems, and a postgraduate path that lets me keep building.
