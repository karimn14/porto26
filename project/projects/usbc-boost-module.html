Executive Summary: Designed a high-density step-up DC-DC converter module stepping up 5V from any standard USB-C port to a regulated 12V 1.5A output for powering routers, lab instruments, and 12V LED drivers. Centered on the CX8823 synchronous boost regulator, the board features a 3.3µH high-current power inductor, 470µF low-ESR solid electrolytic capacitor, input TVS diode surge clamping, and DNP jumper options.

01Design Challenge & Use Case

Field technicians, embedded engineers, and IoT deployers frequently need clean 12V DC power in situations where only 5V USB power banks or laptop ports are available. Inexpensive off-the-shelf boost modules often suffer from poor thermal dissipation, extreme output voltage ripple (>150mV), and lack essential overvoltage/hot-plug protection.

I designed a dedicated, high-efficiency USB-C to 12V step-up power module delivering up to 18W (12V @ 1.5A) with industrial-grade ripple suppression and robust hot-plug surge survivability.

02Power Stage Selection & Inductor Sizing

The switching regulator circuitry is calculated for high efficiency and continuous conduction:

  • CX8823 Synchronous Boost Controller: Operates at high switching frequency (up to 1.2 MHz), reducing required passive component volume while maintaining high conversion efficiency across light to full loads.
  • 3.3 µH Shielded Power Inductor: Sized to maintain continuous conduction mode (CCM) across the 5V to 12V operating window with saturation current rating exceeding 4.5A, preventing inductor core saturation under peak load transients.
  • Low-ESR Output Filtering: Combines a 470 µF low-ESR solid electrolytic capacitor with parallel 10 µF and 100 nF ceramic capacitors to suppress both fundamental switching ripple and high-frequency parasitic ringing, holding output ripple below 25 mV.
Fig 2. Figure 2: Top view of assembled module showing compact surface-mount component placement and thermal copper pour.

03Surge Protection, DNP Options & Thermal Pour

PCB layout and circuit protection highlights:

  • Hot-Plug TVS Surge Clamping: Placed fast-response bidirectional TVS diodes (Z1 / D1) immediately adjacent to the USB-C VBUS pin to clamp inductive voltage spikes caused by cable inductance during hot-plugging.
  • DNP (Do Not Populate) Configuration: Included DNP resistor footprint options in the feedback network, enabling simple one-component configuration for alternative output voltages (9V, 15V, or 19V).
  • High-Current Thermal Layout: Minimized the high di/dt switching loop area by placing the input caps, inductor, switching IC, and output caps in a tight linear flow. Employed 2 oz copper pours with thermal vias to conduct heat away into the ground plane.

04Load Testing & Thermal Verification

Bench validation with electronic DC loads confirmed outstanding power density:

  • Measured conversion efficiency exceeding 92.1% at 12V 1.0A output, maintaining over 88% efficiency at maximum 1.5A continuous output.
  • Output ripple remained below 22 mV peak-to-peak under full 18W load.
  • Thermal equilibrium reached 58 °C under ambient room temperature without forced-air cooling.
