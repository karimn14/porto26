Executive Summary: Engineered a plant sap flow sensor instrument designed to track plant transpiration and water consumption in real time. Designed a high-precision low-noise analog front-end (AFE) conditioning microvolt-level thermocouple probe signals based on the Granier thermal dissipation method (TDM), paired with a customized mathematical DAQ calculation algorithm and a water-resistant 3D modeled tree-mounting enclosure.

01Agronomic Context & Measurement Physics

Monitoring sap flow rate in tree xylem vessels provides direct, quantitative data on plant water stress, transpiration dynamics, and irrigation efficiency. The Granier Thermal Dissipation Method (TDM) uses two needle probes inserted radially into the tree trunk: an upper heated probe and a lower unheated reference probe.

As water moves through the xylem, heat is carried away; the temperature differential (\(\Delta T\)) between the two probes is inversely proportional to sap flux density. Measuring microvolt thermocouple outputs reliably under outdoor agricultural conditions requires extreme analog precision.

Fig 1. Figure 1: Custom analog instrumentation board showing low-noise op-amp filtering, thermocouple connectors, and MCU header.

02Low-Noise Analog Front-End (AFE) Design

The sensor circuit conditions delicate thermoelectric signals:

  • Instrumentation Amplifier Front-End: Integrated low-offset, low-drift rail-to-rail instrumentation amplifiers providing 110 dB+ Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (CMRR) to eliminate 50Hz mains hum and environmental RF pickup.
  • Precision Cold-Junction Compensation: Implemented local RTD/thermistor temperature sensing at the thermocouple connector terminal block to ensure sub-0.05°C temperature differential accuracy.
  • Constant-Current Heater Driver: Designed a regulated low-noise constant-current source driving the upper heating needle with 0.2W continuous dissipation, preventing thermal runaway or measurement drift.
Fig 2. Figure 2: Physical stainless-steel needle thermocouple probes inserted into tree xylem tissue for thermal dissipation tracking.

03Custom DAQ Formula & Weatherproof Enclosure

Firmware calculation and mechanical mounting:

  • Empirical Flow Velocity Equation: Implemented the Granier empirical calibration formula \(u = 0.0119 imes K^{1.231}\) (where \(K = (\Delta T_{max} - \Delta T) / \Delta T\)) directly inside the microcontroller firmware, computing sap flux in liters per hour per square meter.
  • 3D SLA Enclosure Design: Modeled a customized UV-resistant enclosure in SolidWorks, manufactured using SLA resin printing. Features silicone gaskets, conformal cable glands, and tree-trunk curved mounting wings for stable attachment.

04Field Testing & Transpiration Tracking

Field deployment on experimental crops confirmed high sensitivity:

  • Detected sharp diurnal transpiration curves matching sunrise and solar irradiance peaks with under 15-minute response lag.
  • Temperature differential measurement stability achieved ±0.02 °C resolution across outdoor temperature fluctuations from 20 °C to 38 °C.
  • Validated reliable low-power battery operation suitable for solar-assisted remote agro-forestry monitoring.
