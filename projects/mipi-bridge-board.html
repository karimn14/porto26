Executive Summary: Designed a custom 4-layer high-speed interface PCB (45 × 65 mm) converting MIPI DSI display lanes from the ESP32-P4 processor to standard HDMI and LVDS display outputs via the LT8912B bridge IC. Engineered the JLC04161H-7628 controlled-impedance stackup, multi-rail power distribution (1.8V, 3.3V, 5V), 9-channel LBSS138 bi-directional level shifters, and low-capacitance RClamp0524P ESD protection.

01Design Objective & High-Speed Challenges

The Espressif ESP32-P4 RISC-V SoC introduces hardware MIPI DSI interfaces capable of driving high-resolution displays. However, developing industrial and consumer displays often requires interfacing to standard HDMI monitors or LVDS industrial panels.

I engineered a dedicated 45 × 65 mm carrier board centered on the LonTium LT8912B bridge IC. The board bridges 2-lane / 4-lane MIPI DSI inputs (up to 1.5 Gbps per lane) to HDMI 1.4b (up to 1080p @ 60Hz) or single-link LVDS, solving high-speed signal integrity, multi-voltage domain sequencing, and thermal dissipation in an ultra-compact footprint.

024-Layer Stackup & Controlled Impedance Routing

The board utilizes the JLCPCB JLC04161H-7628 4-layer stackup: Layer 1 (Top Signal) — Layer 2 (Solid GND) — Layer 3 (Power Plane) — Layer 4 (Bottom Signal):

  • MIPI DSI Differential Pairs: Routed on Layer 1 over an unbroken Layer 2 GND reference plane with 90 Ω differential impedance (trace width 0.15 mm, gap 0.18 mm). Intra-pair skew was matched to within 0.1 mm to eliminate phase distortion at gigabit data rates.
  • HDMI TMDS Lines: Length-matched 100 Ω differential pairs routed with gentle 45-degree chamfers, avoiding via transitions between layers to prevent impedance discontinuities and return path disruptions.
  • Continuous Ground Planes: Layer 2 is dedicated purely to analog and digital ground return paths, guaranteeing minimum loop area for RF return currents.

03Power Tree, Level Shifters & ESD Protection

Crucial circuit architecture highlights executed on the schematic:

  • Triple-Rail Power Supply Tree: Designed an input filtering and distribution network accepting 5V from USB/Host, stepping down to 3.3V (IO logic) and ultra-low-noise 1.8V (LT8912B analog PLL and core) using dedicated low-dropout regulators (LDOs) with ferrite bead pi-filters (\(\pi\)-filter) to attenuate high-frequency switching hash.
  • Bi-Directional Level Shifting: Implemented a 9-channel level shifter array using LBSS138 N-channel MOSFETs with 10kΩ pull-up resistors to seamlessly interface 1.8V LT8912B sideband controls with 3.3V ESP32-P4 GPIO lines (I2C configuration bus, HPD, CEC).
  • ESD Clamping: Placed low-capacitance (0.3 pF) RClamp0524P TVS diode arrays directly adjacent to the HDMI Type-A connector pins, protecting sensitive differential silicon from ±15kV human-body electrostatic discharge without degrading gigabit eye diagrams.

04Signal Integrity Verification & Output Quality

Validation across lab instrumentation confirmed first-pass fabrication success:

  • Verified clean 1080p @ 60Hz video streaming from ESP32-P4 MIPI DSI framebuffer onto standard HDMI monitors with zero artifacting or line jitter.
  • Measured power rail ripple below 12 mV peak-to-peak on the critical 1.8V PLL supply under full 1.5 Gbps lane transmission.
  • Full pass on DRC/DFM checks with zero unrouted nets, 0.127 mm minimum clearance, and full copper balance across both outer layers.
